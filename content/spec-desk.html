The Studio

The spec desk

Every Dalex game starts as a brief on Malcolm’s desk. Bring him yours: tell him your idea, answer his questions, and take the notes home. They are the first page of the specification a game is built from.

How it works

  • Malcolm asks, you decide. He asks a few numbered questions at a time and suggests an answer to each. Take his suggestion, or say what you want in your own words.
  • Only your game. The desk talks about the game you are designing and nothing else. Games for everyone: if you are young, ask a grown-up to sit with you.
  • Your own idea. Malcolm will not copy a game that already exists or borrow someone else’s characters. Name a game for the feel you are after; the game itself is yours.
  • Take it home. Download your notes whenever you like. Then read the Book to learn how a specification becomes a Spectrum game, or play ours.

Your brief

Malcolm is a character, and his replies are written by an AI model running on the studio’s own computer, not a person. What you type is kept only in your brief, on that computer, so that the conversation can carry on; it is deleted after 30 days, or straight away when you press “Delete my brief”. Please do not type your name, address, email or phone number: the desk removes any it spots. The desk sets no cookies and keeps nothing in your browser; closing or reloading the page ends your session, so download your notes first.

Dalex Software, Malcolm and the rest of the staff are fictional. The games are real, new and original, made for the ZX Spectrum today.
