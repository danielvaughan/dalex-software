The Studio

Every Dalex game is made in the studio on these pages. Gareth shows you round in this five-minute film, and the people at each desk tell you, in their own voices, what they do.

What you will see

Dalex Studio is a software factory. Every desk is a role, every role is carried out by AI agents, and people steer. The film follows a game from the first idea to the magazine review:

  • Commissioning. Malcolm has each idea questioned until it is a proper specification; Colin turns it into requirements, a design and milestones.
  • Production. Mo’s milestone board is kept up to date by agents, and nothing goes out as an issue until a person confirms it. Gareth’s and Yvonne’s screens follow the code being written. Vik checks every build, Emeka plays every verified tape, Derek signs each milestone off, and Omnibot carries the test copies.
  • Assets. Neil, Hyun-joo and Simon make the look, the sprites and the loading screens; Dez writes the music, and his music computer follows his songs on the hi-fi, where Bev is the DJ.
  • Management. Ken estimates what the agents’ work would cost. Estimates, not invoices.
  • INK & PAPER. Brian’s magazine reviews the tape that shipped, independently.
  • A worked example. Gold Master, our game about the studio itself, went from specification to review in one night, and INK & PAPER gave it 72%.
  • For everyone. The studio can be used without reading: pictures for every action, a microphone for questions, and a narrator who reads things aloud.

The footage is the studio itself, running. The voices are synthesised, one for each person, and the music is an original theme.
